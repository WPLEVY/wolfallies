Every resource you need after inheriting a California home, organized in the order you actually need them. Start at Stage 1, work through each stage, and use the tools and guides at each step.

Inheriting a California home means facing dozens of interlocking questions in a short window. What do you do first? What can wait? Who do you need to hire? What deadlines matter?

This kit organizes every Wolf Allies resource into a coherent path. Each stage has one or two guides, checklists, or calculators tailored to that specific point in the process. Start where you are. Move at your own pace. Come back when you need the next tool.

Reference: Key Terms Glossary

Terminology you will encounter throughout the process:

Prop 19
California Proposition 19, effective February 16, 2021. Changed the parent-to-child property tax exclusion. Now requires the inheriting child to make the home their principal residence within 12 months to preserve the parent's assessed value.
Stepped-Up Basis
Federal tax rule that resets the tax basis of inherited property to fair market value at date of death. Substantially reduces capital gains tax when the inheritor later sells.
AB 2016
California Assembly Bill 2016, effective April 1, 2025. Raised the simplified probate threshold for a decedent's primary residence from $184,500 to $750,000.
Petition to Determine Succession to Primary Residence
The court petition (Form DE-310) used to transfer title to a qualifying primary residence under AB 2016 without full probate. Applies to primary residences under $750,000.
IAEA Authority
Independent Administration of Estates Act authority. Allows executors to complete many actions, including selling real property, without individual court approval on each action. Requested when filing the Petition for Probate.
Certification of Trust
A short document summarizing key trust provisions (per California Probate Code Section 18100.5). Used to prove trustee authority without disclosing the entire trust document. Preferred by title companies for trust sales.
Letters Testamentary / Letters of Administration
Court-issued documents formally appointing the executor (with a will) or administrator (without a will) and giving them authority to act on behalf of the estate.
Probate Referee
California-specific court-appointed appraiser used to value estate property in probate cases. Fee is set at 0.1% of property value with a minimum of $75.
BOE-19-P and BOE-19-B
California Board of Equalization forms filed with the county assessor to claim Prop 19 parent-child exclusion. Must be filed timely to preserve the exclusion.
Notice of Proposed Action
Form DE-165 used under IAEA authority to notify beneficiaries of an executor's intended action (like selling real property) with 15 days advance notice, avoiding the need for court confirmation.
