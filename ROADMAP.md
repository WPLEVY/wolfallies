# Roadmap

## Vision

Wolf Allies exists to simplify complex real estate referral and lead generation workflows through the thoughtful application of artificial intelligence.

The long-term objective is to reduce operational friction surrounding professional relationships without replacing the human trust those relationships depend upon.

AI should automate repetitive processes, not replace expertise.

---

## Current Stage

**Private Beta**

Current development is focused on validating the platform with real users while improving reliability, usability, and overall product quality.

Current priorities include:

* Beta user onboarding
* Usage analytics
* User authentication
* Account management
* AI-assisted lead qualification
* Intelligent referral routing
* Production stability
* User feedback collection

## Long-Term Goals

* Multi-state expansion
* Advanced referral intelligence and predictive lead scoring
* AI-assisted business development pipelines
* Multi-agent workflow automation
* Expand into additional professional service industries
* Workflow automation across the complete real estate transaction lifecycle

---

## Guiding Principles

Every product decision is evaluated against five core principles:

* Strengthen professional relationships, not replace them.
* Reduce operational friction.
* Preserve human judgment where trust matters.
* Build simple solutions to complex business problems.
* Let customer and partner feedback guide product development.

---

## Looking Ahead

Wolf Allies is building more than a referral platform.

The long-term vision is to create an intelligent business development platform that helps trusted professionals build stronger relationships, identify opportunities earlier, and spend more time serving clients instead of managing administrative workflows.

Every iteration is guided by measurable user feedback, practical business outcomes, and a commitment to applying AI where it creates genuine value.
