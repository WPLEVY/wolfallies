A curated collection of official California court resources, forms, county recorder offices, and government agencies that trustees, executors, and families commonly need during estate and trust administration. All links go directly to official government or court websites.

California Probate Courts

Probate matters are handled by the Superior Court in the county where the deceased resided. Each county court has its own procedures and forms.

Los Angeles County Superior Court — Probate Division
Probate filings, case search, local rules, and forms for LA County probate matters. Covers the largest probate court in California.

California Judicial Council Forms

The California Judicial Council publishes official court forms used in probate and estate proceedings statewide. These are the forms required by California courts — not optional templates.

Judicial Council Probate Forms (DE series)
All official California probate forms — petitions, inventories, accountings, and orders. Required for probate court filings statewide.
courts.ca.gov
→
California Courts — Probate Self-Help Center
Plain-language guides to California probate procedures, forms, and court processes from the official court system.

County Recorder Offices

County recorder offices handle the recording of deeds and other real property documents. When trust property is sold, the new deed must be recorded with the county recorder in the county where the property is located.

Los Angeles County Recorder
Recording deeds, deed transfers, and real property documents for Los Angeles County properties.
lavote.gov
→
San Diego County Recorder
Recording real property documents and deed transfers for San Diego County.
sdarcc.gov
→
Orange County Recorder
Recording real property documents and deed transfers for Orange County.
ocrecorder.com
→

Property Tax Resources

Property tax reassessment is a critical consideration in estate and trust administration, particularly after Proposition 19 changed the parent-child exclusion rules in 2021.

California Board of Equalization — Proposition 19 Guide
Official BOE guidance on Proposition 19 and its effect on parent-child property tax exclusions for inherited property.
boe.ca.gov
→
LA County Assessor — Change of Ownership Forms
Preliminary Change of Ownership Report (PCOR) and other forms required when real property transfers in LA County.
assessor.lacounty.gov
