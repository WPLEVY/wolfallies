About This Timeline

California trust and probate administration follows a general framework, but the specific tasks, timing, and legal requirements vary based on your situation. This tool generates a customized timeline based on four key factors:

  • Administration type: trust administration, formal probate, or a simplified petition procedure
  • Occupancy status: vacant, occupied by an heir, or occupied by a non-heir
  • Number of beneficiaries: single or multiple beneficiaries
  • Intended outcome: sale, occupancy by one heir, or undecided

The generated timeline includes tasks specific to your combination of these factors. It is intended as a starting framework, not a complete legal guide. Every situation has details this tool cannot capture.

What This Tool Does Not Model

Contested proceedings, complex tax situations, out-of-state property, business interests, unusual asset structures, and California-specific court procedures for your county are not modeled. For any of these, you need direct guidance from a California attorney.
