What to Expect

Common Situations We Route

Families reach out to us for attorney introductions for a range of matters:

  • Opening probate after a family member's death
  • Trust administration and successor trustee questions
  • Selling or transferring inherited real estate
  • Proposition 19 planning and analysis
  • Estate planning to avoid probate for California real estate
  • Family disputes over inherited property
  • Understanding your rights as a beneficiary
  • Federal estate tax questions for larger estates
