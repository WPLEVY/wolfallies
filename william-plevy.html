William B. Plevy is a California-licensed attorney (State Bar of California) and California real estate broker (DRE #01956776) who founded Wolf Allies to help families navigate one of the most challenging intersections in estate law: selling real property held in a trust or probate estate.

Background and Professional Experience

Throughout his career, William has worked on real estate transactions, business structures, and asset ownership matters — regularly encountering the challenges that trustees, executors, and families face when real property is part of an estate or trust. He has maintained professional relationships with trust attorneys, probate attorneys, fiduciaries, and real estate professionals across California.

Working at the intersection of law and real estate, William observed a consistent pattern: families dealing with inherited property, trust-owned real estate, and probate estates were frequently underserved. They encountered decisions with significant legal and financial consequences — often while grieving — without adequate guidance or access to professionals who actually understood this specific type of transaction.

Cash investors approached them immediately with below-market offers. General residential agents who had never handled a trust sale accepted listings without understanding the documentation requirements. Families who needed a specialist too often worked with a generalist.

Wolf Allies was built to close that gap.

Why William Built Wolf Allies

The dual background — law and real estate brokerage — gave William a distinctive vantage point. He understood both the legal framework that governs trust and probate real estate (fiduciary duties, court confirmation, IAEA authority, disclosure obligations) and the practical real estate dynamics (pricing strategy, agent selection, marketing, negotiation, escrow).

The insight that shaped Wolf Allies: families in these situations need access to both — someone who understands the legal context and can connect them with the right real estate professional for their specific market and situation. Not a call center. Not a generic directory. A curated connection to a specialist who handles these transactions every day.

Wolf Allies makes that connection — and provides the educational resources that help families understand what they're facing before they pick up the phone.
