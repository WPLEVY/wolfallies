The Problem We Solve

Most families have never done this before.

Inheriting a home, taking on trustee responsibilities, or navigating a probate estate is something most people encounter once — if ever. There is no rehearsal. The decisions are real, the timelines are often compressed, and the financial stakes are significant.

At the same time, the real estate industry is not designed for this. Most agents handle standard residential transactions. Trust and probate sales have specific legal requirements, documentation standards, disclosure obligations, and fiduciary considerations that a general residential agent may never have encountered.

The result: families make consequential real estate decisions with inadequate guidance, sometimes leaving significant money on the table or creating legal exposure they didn't know existed.

Wolf Allies closes that gap. We connect trustees, executors, heirs, and families with California real estate specialists who handle trust, probate, and estate property sales every day — and we provide the educational resources to help families understand what they're facing.

Wolf Allies is a California-licensed real estate referral platform. When you submit your information, we identify the agent in our network who has the deepest relevant experience for your specific city and situation. That agent calls you.

We do not represent you in the transaction. We do not provide legal advice. We connect you with the professional who does — and we provide educational resources to help you understand what you're facing before and during the process.

Under California Business and Professions Code, a real estate agent cannot charge a seller more because of a broker referral arrangement. Your commission is identical whether you found your agent through Wolf Allies or on your own. Our involvement has zero financial impact on you.
